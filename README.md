# University Wi-Fi Satisfaction Analysis

## Project Overview

This project evaluates students' satisfaction with university Wi-Fi services using a questionnaire-based survey of 50 students.

The study examines internet speed, signal strength, connection stability, Wi-Fi usage, and overall student satisfaction.

## Objectives

- Evaluate students' satisfaction with university Wi-Fi facilities.
- Identify major problems affecting Wi-Fi performance.
- Examine the impact of Wi-Fi issues on academic activities.
- Suggest improvements to campus internet services.

## Methodology

- **Research Approach:** Quantitative survey
- **Sample Size:** 50 students
- **Data Collection:** Structured questionnaire
- **Analysis:** Descriptive statistics, frequencies, percentages, and mean scores
- **Software:** Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets

## Key Findings

- **Wi-Fi Signal Strength:** Mean score of 3.02/5
- **Internet Speed:** Mean score of 2.98/5
- **Connection Stability:** Mean score of 2.72/5
- **Overall Satisfaction:** Mean score of 3.00/5

The findings indicate that students experience problems with internet speed, network stability, and coverage.

## Recommendations

- Expand Wi-Fi coverage across campus.
- Improve internet bandwidth.
- Install additional access points.
- Introduce better network monitoring and technical support.

## Project Files

- **Academic Report:** Detailed analysis, statistical tables, and visualizations.
- **Presentation:** Summary of the study, findings, and recommendations.
